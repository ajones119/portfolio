# Mission: Atmospheric Three.js Scenes

## Why
Build an evolving interactive forest for the portfolio while learning to make simple geometry feel atmospheric through deliberate lighting, shadows, fog, and color.

## Success looks like
- Explain what each light contributes to the forest image.
- Tune sun direction, fill light, fog, shadow coverage, and exposure without guessing.
- Reuse the lighting stack in later Three.js scenes.

## Constraints
- Learn against the code in this repository.
- Prefer short, practical lessons tied to visible results.
- Keep the initial forest asset-free and desktop-first.

## Out of scope
- Physically accurate outdoor-light simulation.
- Advanced volumetric lighting and post-processing for now.
