Atmosphere lighting stack

A compact recipe for making simple Three.js geometry read as a warm, misty outdoor scene.

Warm directional sun

Use DirectionalLight for parallel daylight. Place it high and far to one side. A lower angle stretches shadows.

Cool hemisphere fill

Give upward faces a cool sky color and downward faces a dark ground color. This preserves form inside shadows.

Fog + matching background

Use one color for both. Linear fog leaves the foreground clear, then compresses distant trees into soft layers.

Tone mapping + exposure

ACES filmic tone mapping rolls bright sunlight into a pleasing range. Exposure controls the whole final image.
